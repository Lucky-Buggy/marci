Know the area

The Pahrump Valley

Pahrump is an unincorporated town at the southern tip of Nye County, Nevada, set in a high-desert valley with the Spring Mountains to the east. It sits roughly an hour’s drive west of Las Vegas along Nevada State Route 160, and the valley has grown steadily as more people have made it home.

Area facts: U.S. Census Bureau (2020) and Wikipedia, “Pahrump, Nevada.”
